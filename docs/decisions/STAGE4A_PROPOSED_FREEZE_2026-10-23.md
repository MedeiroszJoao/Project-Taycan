## Scope of what can be finished

4A is a **synthetic data production contract**, separate from empirical validity / robustness (4B). It is not a real-Taycan diagnostic and cannot assert battery pack physical fidelity. The original Stage 3 model/freeze, original Stage 4 manifest, 1152 test and 2232 official planned episodes are untouched.

1. **Physics choice:** `src/simulator/candidate_sim/model_4a_activity_gated.py`, derived with an explicit change from `model_energy_consistent.py`. I01 additional fault loss `1600W * severity` is injected **only if both rotor RPM and torque are nonzero**, during traction or regenerative torque. No additional I01 loss at rest/parked charge. This is a DECLARED model assumption to focus on switching/operation fault losses, not a verified Taycan standby power law; ordinary baseline auxiliary power remains modeled. The prior always-on I01 and all Pilot 2 data remain historically reproducible.
2. **Observation:** `src/observation/observation_layer_candidate.py` is a reconstructed **replacement candidate**, not the unrecovered historical observation original. It consumes only approved synthetic OBD channels (not latent fault truth); the four seeded sources are physics/observer/aux/nuisance; an independent degradation seed is restricted to exploratory robustness. No unit can silently rename it the "original".
3. **Feature contract:** the preexisting immutable 28 observed features +28 availability masks in `scripts/run_stage4_experiments.py`. They remain unchanged after Pilot 2. F03 already contains the torque-squared coefficient. F15,F16,F17 require complete 84-module-temperature-channel series, and are masked when any required module sensor is missing; nominal full coverage in 4A, quantization/delay/dropout are explicitly stressors of 4B. This is an architectural limitation, not a closed-world accuracy conclusion.
4. **Research-only duty:** six one-hour cycles of 12min cruise, 12min acceleration, 12min regen, 12min rest, 12min parked charge; 5 s step, 4320 steps/6h. Toy torque/RPM are assumed, not a measured drive trace. Charging at HV DC bus is an idealized power input, without real charger/taper limits. NOMINAL power 47.8046875kW; SHIFTED cold regen 31.5625kW; both were selected using prior development tuning, **not** historical registered 0..120kW bisection. For new official protocol, pin these as prospective coefficients or rerun approved bisection, no retrospective claims.
5. **4A candidate smoke:** 12 scenarios, one per H00/B01..B09/M01/I01, alternating nominal/shifted, severity 0.25 for faults, H00 0. Fresh independent 4-stream seeds disjoint from the official manifest. Fail closed on any exception, NaN/inf, unrecognized signal, missing feature/mask contract, any latent branch SOC outside 30..80%, absolute DC algebraic power closure >1e-5 W, fixed-coefficient RC ledger absolute residual >1e-4 J/step, absent motoring/parked modes, or excess I01 losses in parked modes. Record all 12 results, min/max SOC over **all** branch states, terminal mean SOC, integrated HV charge and maximum energy residual.
6. **Physics caveat:** closed DC power algebra and exact constant-coefficient 1RC identity do NOT imply whole-pack first-law conservation when aging, variable RC capacitance and electrochemical/entropic work have no constitutive model. These remain explicit model-domain limits. 4A permits only reproducibility and local consistency claims, NOT general physical conservation.
7. **No official smoke authorization:** this 12x6h run is a *prospective candidate QC dry run*. It does not unseal or replace the formally registered 12-episode smoke and the 2232 official campaign. The original hash-gated runner cannot be bypassed. A newly approved versioned methodology must be issued before declaring official Stage4 release.

## Adversarial correction after first 12x6h candidate smoke (2026-10-23)

The first independent 12-class six-hour smoke on the *previous* observer passed **12/12** (GitHub Actions run `40026083703`, 96/96 unit tests), but the unsealed H00 nominal run finished at **71.9842% mean SOC** rather than the nominal calibrated 60%. Investigation reproduced the cause: `aux_cap_W=283.28194 W` for that episode, and the historical exploratory observer clipped the *entire* auxiliary DC load to that value despite the assumed 1900 W baseline. For `cap<1900 W`, every sample was saturated, turning an intended stochastic nuisance into an almost deterministic low-load episode. This was NOT an error in the simulator's local DC algebra; it was a consequential ambiguity in the auxiliary-cap interpretation. It must not be concealed by a PASS on the 30–80% SOC window.

**Prospective remedy (not retroactive):** preserve `observation_layer_candidate.py` and all Pilot 2 data exactly. Introduce separate `src/observation/observation_layer_4a_candidate.py`: `aux_cap_W` now bounds the **OU residual** about the nominal 1900 W load; actual load is `max(0,1900+clamped_residual)`, with hypothetical upper bound `1900+cap` W. This is a new, explicit assumption, not the historical preregistered meaning and not a measured Taycan specification. `scripts/run_stage4a_candidate_smoke.py` must record the actual mean/SD auxiliary power, integrated auxiliary energy, residual cap, observer SOC clipping, all 28 feature masks, hidden SOC window and local energy residuals. Source hashes and all 12 independent six-hour episodes MUST be regenerated after this prospective change; earlier 12/12 cannot attest to the corrected observer.

**Pilot 2 analytical QC:** 2916/2916 development runs PASS; 27,720 marginal pair/feature cells; 7087/27720 (25.57%) unestimable, 397/27720 (1.43%) missing bootstrap CI. Mean available features: 28.00/28 nominal, 21.00/28 under assumed realistic degradation, 17.83/28 under assumed severe degradation. F15–F17 are completely unavailable in both degraded levels because the all-sensors-required mask policy is strict. These results are descriptive and **do not** establish 5% FPR / 80% power, out-of-generator robustness or classification accuracy.

**Remaining physical limitations:** the original six-hour H00 reference charging powers are development-derived; they cannot be backdated into the sealed 0–120 kW calibration. Time-varying chemical capacity and implicit RC capacitance still have no independent constitutive work ledger. The activity-gated I01 matched-energy severities for a unit M01 severity are **0.09882981940668557 nominal** and **0.10643684785638745 shifted**, an identity of assumed injection functions only; the old always-on values are not valid for the new gate. The prospective 12-combination analytic test does not validate a Porsche inverter.

The branch remains **DRAFT / UNAPPROVED**. No official heldout test episodes, Stage 5 fitting, merge, or real-vehicle diagnostic authorization.
