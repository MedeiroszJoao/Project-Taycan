# 2026-10-23 — Stage 4 non-scoring development extension

**Scope:** Only a new development-only experiment, not a rewrite or implicit permission to open Stage 4's 1,152 reserved test episodes. A classifier may be prototyped later as an expressly exploratory Stage 5 precursor, never reported as a preregistered Stage 5 result. This record is prospective relative to all new `TAYCAN-STAGE4-UNSEALED-DEVONLY-20261023-PAIRWISE-v1` draws.

**Immediate new exploratory experiment:** 12 hypotheses × 16 distinct independent episodes = 192 ten-minute toy runs at severity 0.5 (H00=0), fixed nominal signed torque waveform, with 4 newly generated 32-bit seeds each. The derivation is SHA-256 namespace + hypothesis + replicate + stream, collision-checked against all 8,928 existing frozen seeds. No labels or samples from any reserved validation/test episode are consumed. Nuisance scales are newly drawn from the already recorded development ranges. Both code and results are labeled `UNSEALED_DEVELOPMENT_ONLY`.

**Diagnostics, no fit:** enumerate 66 unordered hypothesis pairs, report each of the 28 marginal feature separations using Fisher mean-difference ratio `(mu1-mu2)^2/(s1²+s2²)` and **symmetric Gaussian KL / Jeffreys** `(KL(N1||N2)+KL(N2||N1))` under explicitly labeled marginal Gaussian proxies. Unestimable zero variance and n<8 produce null, not artificial infinity. These are descriptive, biased low-n estimates; they are not classifiers, AUC, detection thresholds, posterior probabilities, multivariate joint KL, verified B02/B07 distinction, or vehicle accuracy. They do not provide a physically independent generator B.

**Methodological gap:** The existing manifesto has 2,232 unique episode IDs, 8,928 noncolliding seeds, and no vehicle/session ID. Reuse of a numeric `replicate` across splits is NOT evidence of shared seeds or leaked samples; no leakage was detected by the seed audit. No evaluation of per-vehicle longitudinal change is possible without a separate hierarchical design with vehicle_id/session_id and grouped partitions. Such a new design must not reinterpret existing episodes as vehicles.

**Independent generator B proposal, not implemented or calibrated:** use third-party PyBaMM SPMe/DFN cell electrochemistry and thermal, frozen parameters/inputs before evaluating transfer. PyBaMM does not independently generate all motor/inverter/UDS channels, so a B battery-only external benchmark cannot by itself validate the project's 12-way whole-vehicle classification. Define a scope-limited battery fault and measured electrical/thermal-channel mapping, or obtain genuinely independent motor/inverter observations. Protocol/dataset generation must remain independent of model A's features and results. PyBaMM model docs: https://docs.pybamm.org/en/v26.4.2/source/examples/notebooks/models/compare-lithium-ion.html
