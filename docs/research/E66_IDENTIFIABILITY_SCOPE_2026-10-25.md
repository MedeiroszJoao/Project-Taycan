# Scope of the conditional kinetic fit

The single fitted multiplier scales both exchange-current functions. A minimum of this one-dimensional objective establishes only a conditional optimum with the other parameters fixed. It does not identify individual electrode kinetics, E66 active material geometry or diffusion coefficients. Three pulses share one cell/session, so neither their voltage samples nor the pulses justify an independent-cell confidence interval.

Primary literature checked 2026-10-25:

- Bizeray et al., *Identifiability and parameter estimation of the single particle lithium-ion battery model*, https://arxiv.org/abs/1702.02471, DOI 10.1109/TCST.2018.2838097: their structural SPM result requires assumptions on electrode OCV gradients and combines kinetics. It is not a proof for our SPMe fit.
- Laue, Röder and Krewer, *Practical identifiability of electrochemical P2D models for lithium-ion batteries*, https://doi.org/10.1007/s10800-021-01579-5: their case study documents parameter ambiguities with OCP/C-rate data and the value of richer dynamic/electrode-resolved characterization. It does not establish impossibility for every reduced model.

For this project, fit quality, mathematical identifiability under restrictions, transfer to another session and external vehicle diagnostic validity are four separate claims. None implies all the others.
